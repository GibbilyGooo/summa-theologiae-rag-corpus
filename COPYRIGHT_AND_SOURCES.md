# Copyright, Sources, and Attribution

## Underlying work

St. Thomas Aquinas's *Summa Theologiae* is in the public domain.

The English text represented here is the translation by the Fathers of the English Dominican Province, published in historical volumes during the early twentieth century. The translation is treated as public domain in the United States.

## Digital witnesses

The article-section layer was normalized from the following primary digital witness:

- New Advent, *Summa Theologiae*: https://www.newadvent.org/summa/

New Advent identifies the source as the Second and Revised Edition, 1920, translated by the Fathers of the English Dominican Province. New Advent also displays separate copyright notices for its online edition and website. Users should review those notices and applicable law for their jurisdiction.

The following were used as comparison and recovery witnesses:

- Christian Classics Ethereal Library (CCEL), Benziger Bros. 1947 edition: https://ccel.org/a/aquinas/summa/home.html
- Public-domain historical volume scans preserved by Internet Archive, identified individually in `provenance/source_research_log.md` and `provenance/recovery_manifest.json`

The repository does not reproduce New Advent's website code, visual design, advertisements, navigation, or editorial pages. It contains normalized records of the historical translation, canonical-locus metadata, and independently recovered prologues.
